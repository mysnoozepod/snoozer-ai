# Pressure Mapping Test Notes

## Purpose

This file captures future pressure mapping test notes.

Pressure mapping is not the first implementation target. The first target is zone awareness.

This document exists so the team can organize pressure mapping questions without letting it take over Phase 1.

## Test Readiness Checklist

Before pressure mapping tests begin, the team must confirm:

- The pod is non-adjustable.
- The pressure sensor pad can fit safely.
- The sensor does not affect comfort too much.
- The sensor can be powered safely.
- The sensor can be mounted or positioned consistently.
- Cables can be secured.
- Readings are understandable.
- Language avoids medical claims.
- Ty approves the test scope.

## Approved Interpretation

Pressure maps can support general explanations such as:

- More pressure in one area
- Less pressure in one area
- More even distribution
- Comparison between two mattress builds

Pressure maps cannot support medical conclusions.

## Future Snoozer Use

Possible future Snoozer explanations:

- “This view helps show where your body is applying more pressure.”
- “The goal is not to diagnose anything. It is to compare comfort and support feel.”
- “We can use this as one more input while comparing your pod options.”

## Final Rule

Pressure mapping stays parked until zone awareness is stable and Ty approves the next phase.
