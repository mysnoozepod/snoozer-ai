# Pressure Mapping Scope

## Purpose

This document defines the approved scope for pressure mapping in Sensor Operating Layer v1.

Pressure mapping is useful, but it must be controlled. It is not part of every pod and it must not become a medical claim system.

## Approved Scope

Pressure mapping is approved only for the two non-adjustable-base pods.

Pressure mapping is not approved for pods with motion or adjustable bases in v1.

## Why Adjustable Base Pods Are Excluded

Adjustable bases introduce mechanical movement, bending, lifting, and positioning changes.

That creates issues for pressure sensor pads, including:

- Fit problems
- Comfort problems
- Cable strain
- Reading inconsistency
- Pad movement
- Durability concerns
- Maintenance complexity

For v1, pressure mapping stays on non-adjustable pods only.

## Approved Use

Pressure mapping may be used to help explain:

- Pressure distribution
- Comfort differences
- Support differences
- How a shopper’s body interacts with different mattress builds
- General comparison between approved test pods

## Not Approved

Pressure mapping must not be used to:

- Diagnose medical conditions
- Claim to fix pain
- Claim to treat sleep disorders
- Prove spinal correction
- Replace professional medical advice
- Make final product decisions without approved rules
- Store sensitive raw maps without a privacy policy

## Approved Language Direction

Allowed:

- “This may help show how pressure is distributed.”
- “This can help compare comfort and support differences.”
- “This gives Snoozer another way to explain how different builds may feel.”

Not allowed:

- “This will fix your back.”
- “This proves your spine is aligned.”
- “This diagnoses your sleep issue.”
- “This mattress treats your condition.”

## Phase 1 Pressure Mapping Goal

The Phase 1 goal is only to define the scope and testing approach.

Pressure mapping implementation comes later.

## Open Questions

- Which two pods are the non-adjustable-base pods?
- What pressure sensor product will be evaluated?
- How will pressure data be displayed?
- Will pressure maps be stored or viewed only during the session?
- What privacy language is needed?
- What disclaimers are needed?
- How will pressure results connect to Snoozer explanations?

## Final Rule

Pressure mapping is an education and comparison tool. It is not a medical device and not a shortcut around approved product guidance.
