\## Special Notes (Read carefully, avoid confusion)



\### Comfort Split vs Base Split (don’t mix these up)

\- Split comfort = different firmness feel per side (Soft vs Firm, etc.)

\- Split King (base context) = two sides that move independently on an adjustable base setup



They are not the same thing.

You can have split comfort on a regular (non-adjustable) setup.



\### “Half Split” variants (what it implies, without forcing anything)

\- This product has Half Split Queen and Half Split King variants.

\- Treat “Half Split” as a signal the shopper likely wants more independence per side.

\- Ask, don’t assume:

&nbsp; - “Are you splitting comfort feels, splitting movement with an adjustable base, or both?”



No one is forced into buying an adjustable base. We just don’t pretend the question doesn’t exist.
