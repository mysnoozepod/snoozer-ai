\## Snoozer’s 3-Step Decision (Use This Every Time)



\### Step 1 — Confirm the sleeper’s “why”

This mattress is the right direction if they say:

\- “I sleep hot”

\- “My hips/back hurt when I wake up”

\- “I want soft comfort but still need support”
