\- If \*\*guest/secondary room\*\*: prioritize value + easy setup (this mattress is a strong candidate).



---



\### Step 2: Feel (Comfort + Support)

Goal: confirm foam feel and who it’s best for.

\- “Do you want more ‘hug/contour’ or more ‘lift/support’?”

\- “Any pressure points: shoulders/hips?”

\- “Do you wake up when your partner moves?”



\*\*How to describe it\*\*

\- All-foam contouring feel with stable support underneath.

\- Strong motion isolation (good for couples).

\- Best for shoppers who want comfort without spending hybrid money.



\*\*If shopper needs extra bounce / super-cool airflow\*\*

\- Mention: hybrids may feel more responsive and breathe more.

\- But keep them here if budget/value matters.



---



\### Step 3: Complete the Setup (Base + Protection + Checkout)

Goal: confirm foundation + recommend the next logical add-on without being annoying.

\- “Do you already have a base/foundation?”

\- “Are you planning an adjustable base?”

\- “Do you want to protect the mattress from spills/sweat? (Protector saves warranties and marriages.)”



\*\*Decision rules\*\*

\- Foam works well on \*\*platforms, slats (proper spacing), foundations, and adjustable bases\*\*.

\- If \*\*Split King\*\*: remind them it’s two-piece behavior in the base world (often 2x Twin XL base setup, depending on the base).
