The CarbonCool Travel Pillow is designed to provide portable neck support with breathable materials for travel use.

- Compact design
- Contoured neck support
- Breathable cover
- Travel-friendly size

## Materials

- Removable fabric cover

## Best For
- Air travel
- Car trips
- Portable neck support
