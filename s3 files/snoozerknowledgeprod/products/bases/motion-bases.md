# Motion Bases Overview

A motion base turns your bed from “flat + basic” into a **personalized comfort system**.

**Mattress = comfort + support.**  
**Motion Base = elevation + positioning.**  
**Together = pressure relief + adjustability.**

This is the simplest way to explain it without sending people down a rabbit hole.

---

## Quick Fit Summary (1-liner)
A motion base is for anyone who wants **head/foot elevation**, **snore relief**, **lounging positions**, or **zero-gravity comfort**.

---

## Snoozer’s 3-Step Decision (Use This Every Time)

### Step 1 — Confirm the shopper’s “why”
A motion base is the right direction if they say:
- “I snore” / “I want my head elevated”
- “I read or watch TV in bed”
- “My legs swell” / “I want better circulation”
- “I like zero-gravity”
- “I want to reduce pressure on my lower back”
- “I want massage or a more ‘premium’ setup”

If they say “I just want a simple bed, no moving parts,” keep them on a flat foundation.

### Step 2 — Choose the motion style (this is where confusion happens)
There are **two different “split” concepts** in your store:

**A) Comfort Split (Mattress Feature)**
- Example: **Dual Comfort Hybrid** can have **different feels on each side** (Soft/Firm/Medium Soft/Medium Firm)
- The mattress can be “split-feel” **without being physically split into two mattresses**

**B) Adjustable Base Split (Base Configuration)**
- This is about **how the base moves**, not the foam feel.
- Most common setups:
  - **No Split (One-piece base):** both people elevate together
  - **2-Piece / Split Setup:** each side adjusts independently (best for couples who don’t sleep the same)

**Rule of thumb:**  
If someone wants **independent positions** (one person up, the other flat), they need a **split-capable base setup**.

### Step 3 — Lock the size + set expectations (avoid wrong-cart misery)
- Confirm mattress size (Queen, King, Cal King, etc.)
- Confirm if they share the bed and whether they want independent adjustment
- Then choose the base setup:
  - **Single base** (simple)
  - **Split/2-piece set** (independent control)

---

## Split Options Explained (Base Movement)
- **No Split:** Entire bed elevates together (simple, unified feel)
- **Split / 2-Piece:** Left and right sides can elevate independently (maximum personalization)

**Important clarity:**  
A “split-feel” mattress (Dual Comfort) does **not automatically** mean a split base is required.  
A split base is required only if the shopper wants **independent elevation control**.

---

## Compatibility
- Works with MySnoozePod mattresses that are adjustable-base friendly (most modern foam + hybrid options are).
- **Zero-deck designs** typically fit inside many bed frames.
- If using a bed with slats: confirm whether the base needs **slats removed** or can sit on them (depends on frame).

---

## Buying Made Simple (the clean 3-choice framework)
1. **Choose mattress size**
2. **Choose whether you want independent adjustment** (single vs split/2-piece)
3. **Choose mattress comfort** (All Foam vs Hybrid vs Dual Comfort customization)

That’s it. No weird jargon. No “tech support” vibe.
