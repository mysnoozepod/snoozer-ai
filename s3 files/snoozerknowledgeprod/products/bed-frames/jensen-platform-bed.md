# Jensen Platform Bed

## Overview
The Jensen Platform Bed is a low-profile platform frame designed to support a mattress without the need for a box spring or foundation.

## Key Features
- Slatted support system
- Sturdy frame construction
- Modern platform design
- Compatible with most mattress types

## Materials
- Solid wood frame
- Reinforced support slats

## Best For
- Platform mattress setups
- Bedrooms with a minimalist aesthetic
- Users seeking a low-profile frame

## Care Instructions
Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid excessive moisture and harsh cleaning agents.
