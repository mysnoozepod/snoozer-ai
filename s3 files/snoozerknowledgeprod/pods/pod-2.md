## What’s on display
SnoozePod 2 features the **12&quot; Dual Comfort** in a **Split King** paired with the **Premium Motion Adjustable Base**.  
Left side is **Medium Soft**, right side is **Medium Firm**.

## Best for
- Couples who want **split comfort** but not extreme soft/firm.
- Guests who want a **balanced feel**: cushioned, but still supportive.
- People who toss/turn and need **easy repositioning**.

## Quick test (2 minutes)
1) **Side (60s):** should feel cushioned, not crushed.  
2) **Back (45s):** check lumbar support and hip sink.  
3) **Roll (15s):** should feel stable and easy to move.

## Adjustable base quick routine
- **Zero Gravity (60s)**
- **Head up slightly (30s)**
- **Feet up slightly (30s)**

## What to say when asked “how is this different from Pod 1?”
“Pod 1 is **Soft/Firm**. Pod 2 is more **balanced**: Medium Soft vs Medium Firm. Same Dual Comfort concept, less extreme ends.”

## Notes for Snoozer
- If guest wants “not too soft” → Medium Firm.
- If guest wants “pressure relief but not mushy” → Medium Soft.
- For checkout/cart intent, add **12-dual-comfort-hybrid** and optionally **premium-motion-adjustable-base**.
