## What’s on display
SnoozePod 4 features the **12&quot; All Foam** in a **Queen** paired with the **Premium Motion Adjustable Base**.

## Best for
- Side sleepers who want **pressure relief** at shoulder/hip.
- Guests sensitive to **motion transfer** (sharing the bed).
- People who prefer a more **hugging foam feel**.

## Quick test (2 minutes)
1) **Side (60s):** shoulder and hip pressure should ease.  
2) **Back (45s):** feel supported, not bowed.  
3) **Roll (15s):** should feel controlled, but foam may be less “bouncy” than hybrid.

## Adjustable base mini routine
- **Zero Gravity (60s)**: pressure relief + back comfort
- **Head up slightly (30s)**: breathing/reading comfort

## What to say when asked “what’s the vibe of this bed?”
“This is our **all-foam pressure relief** setup: it absorbs movement and contours more than the hybrid.”
