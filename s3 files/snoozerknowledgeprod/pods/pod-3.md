## What’s on display
SnoozePod 3 features the **14&quot; Hybrid** in a **King** paired with a **Platform Base**.

## Best for
- Guests who want **more support + easier movement**.
- Combination sleepers who roll and reposition frequently.
- People who like a little **bounce** (instead of deep foam hug).
- Anyone comparing foam vs hybrid feel.

## Quick test (2 minutes)
1) **Normal position (60s):** feel should be supported and responsive.  
2) **Switch position (45s):** pressure should stay controlled.  
3) **Edge check (15s):** sit/edge feel should stay stable.

## What to say when asked “what does hybrid mean?”
“Hybrid means you get a more **supportive, responsive feel** and generally easier movement, with comfort layers on top.”

## Notes for Snoozer
- If guest says “I feel stuck in foam” → this is the counter-option.
- If guest wants maximum pressure relief (very sensitive shoulders/hips) → compare to Pod 4/5 all-foam.
- For checkout/cart intent, add **14-hybrid** and/or **platform-base**.
