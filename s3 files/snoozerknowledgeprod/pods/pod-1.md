## What’s on display
SnoozePod 1 features the **12&quot; Dual Comfort** in a **Half Split King** paired with the **Premium Motion Adjustable Base**.  
Left side is **Soft**, right side is **Firm**.

## Best for
- Couples who want **different feels** without buying two beds.
- Side sleepers who want **pressure relief** (Soft side).
- Back sleepers who want **support + alignment** (Firm side).
- Guests who want to test **Zero Gravity** and head/foot elevation.

## Quick test (2 minutes)
1) **Side (60s):** shoulder + hip pressure should *diffuse*, not spike.  
2) **Back (45s):** lower back should feel supported; hips shouldn’t “drop.”  
3) **Roll (15s):** movement should feel controlled, not stuck.

## Deep test (7 minutes)
- Spend longer on your primary position, then switch.
- Toggle base positions: **Flat → Zero Gravity → slight head up**.

## What to say when asked “what bed is this?”
“This is our **Dual Comfort** setup. One side is softer for pressure relief, the other is firmer for support, and the adjustable base lets you find your best position.”

## Notes for Snoozer
- If guest mentions **shoulder pinch** → steer them to **Soft side**.
- If guest mentions **lower back** support → steer them to **Firm side** + Zero Gravity.
- If guest says “add it to cart” → add **12-dual-comfort-hybrid** and/or **premium-motion-adjustable-base** depending on what they confirmed.
