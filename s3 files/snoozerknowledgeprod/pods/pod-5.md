## What’s on display
SnoozePod 5 features the **10&quot; All Foam** in a **Queen** paired with a **Storage Base**.

- Guests who want **simple comfort** without extra complexity.

2) **Switch position (45s):** check if pressure builds quickly or stays controlled.  
3) **Edge/sit (15s):** basic stability check.
