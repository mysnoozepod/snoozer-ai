## Overview
We respect your privacy and handle all customer data with care.  
This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information gathered online, in the showroom, and through Snoozer interactions.

---

## What We Collect
- Name, contact information, and delivery address  
- Purchase and payment details (securely processed)  
- Snoozer chat interactions for service improvement  
- Optional marketing preferences and reward participation

---

## How We Use Data
- To process orders, deliveries, and support requests  
- To improve your showroom and Snoozer experience  
- To share relevant promotions only if you opt in  

---

## Data Security
All sensitive data is encrypted and stored securely.  
We never sell or share personal information with unauthorized third parties.

---

## Cookies & Analytics
Our website uses cookies and anonymized analytics to improve performance.  
You can disable cookies anytime through your browser settings.

## Effective Date
This policy is effective as of **October 2025** and may be updated at any time.
