## Overview
We partner with trusted local carriers to deliver your products quickly and safely.  
Standard delivery times range from **3–7 business days** from purchase, depending on location and product availability.

---

## Delivery Options
- **Standard Delivery**: Front-door or threshold delivery at no charge for qualifying orders.  
- **White-Glove Setup**: In-room setup, assembly, and packaging removal.  
- **Old Mattress Removal**: Available upon request during delivery.

---

## Delivery Fees
- Free for orders **$999+** within our local service area.  
- A small delivery fee may apply to orders below that threshold or outside standard regions.

---

## Scheduling & Tracking
After your purchase, you’ll receive a scheduling message via text or email.  
You can track or reschedule deliveries directly through Snoozer or by contacting our support team.

---

## Missed Deliveries
If you miss your delivery appointment, we’ll reschedule at no extra charge.  
Repeat missed appointments may incur a rescheduling fee.

---

## Effective Date
This policy is effective as of **October 2025** and may be updated at any time.
