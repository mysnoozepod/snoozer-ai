# Warranty – MySnoozePod  

## Mattress Warranty  
- Mattresses are covered by a **10-year limited warranty**.  
- Covers defects in workmanship or materials, including:  
  - Sagging greater than 1.5 inches not caused by improper use.  
  - Manufacturing defects in foam or cover materials.  
- Excludes normal wear and tear, stains, or damage caused by misuse.  

## Motion Base Warranty  
- Motion bases include a **10-year limited warranty**.  
- First year: **full coverage**, including parts, labor, and shipping.  
- Years 2–10: **limited coverage**, parts only; labor and shipping fees may apply.  
- Coverage applies only when bases are used with compatible mattresses.  

## Exclusions  
- Improper use, abuse, or modifications void warranty.  
- Coverage does not extend to incidental or consequential damages.  
- Commercial use not covered.  

## How to Claim  
- Contact MySnoozePod Customer Care with proof of purchase and photos of the issue.  
- Warranty service may involve repair, replacement, or substitution with a comparable product.
