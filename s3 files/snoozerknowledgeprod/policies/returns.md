## Overview
All mattresses include a **100-night sleep trial** to ensure complete satisfaction.  
If you’re not happy with your mattress during this time, you can return or exchange it once at no charge within our standard service area.

---

## Eligibility
- Applies to **mattress purchases only**.  
- Returns or exchanges must occur within **100 nights** from delivery.  
- Mattresses must be in good condition and free from stains, damage, or abuse.  
- Only one exchange is permitted per customer per purchase.

---

## Non-Returnable Items
- Motion bases and adjustable frames  
- Bedding (sheets, protectors, comforters)  
- Pillows and accessories  
These items are **final sale** once opened or delivered due to hygiene and electrical component safety.

---

## Refund Process
- Refunds are issued within **3–5 business days** after pickup.  
- The original payment method will be used unless otherwise arranged.  
- Any returns outside our primary service area may incur a small pickup fee.

---

## How to Start a Return
Contact us through **Snoozer**, by phone, or in-store.  
We’ll arrange pickup and confirm your refund once the mattress is received.

---

## Effective Date
This policy is effective as of **October 2025** and may be updated at any time.
