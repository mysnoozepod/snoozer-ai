## ❓ How long does delivery take?
Most orders arrive within **3–7 business days** after purchase.  
We’ll confirm your delivery date by text or email once your order is ready to ship.

---

## ❓ Who handles delivery?
Deliveries are coordinated through our trusted local logistics partners.  
They’re trained to handle every item with care and respect your space.

---

## ❓ Do you offer setup or installation?
Yes — you can add **White-Glove Setup** at checkout.  
Our team will bring your items to the room of choice, unpack, assemble, and remove packaging.

---

## ❓ Will you remove my old mattress?
Absolutely.  
Old mattress removal is available during delivery upon request.  
We’ll handle disposal responsibly and safely.

---

## ❓ Is delivery free?
Delivery is **free for orders over $999** within our local service area.  
Orders below that amount or outside our region may include a small delivery fee.

---

## ❓ How do I track my delivery?
You’ll receive a tracking link by text or email when your order is scheduled.  
You can also check the status through Snoozer by asking, *“Track my delivery.”*

---

## ❓ What if I miss my delivery window?
No worries — we’ll contact you to reschedule at no extra cost.  
You can also reach out through Snoozer or call our delivery support line.

---

## ❓ Do you deliver outside the local area?
Yes, extended-area delivery is available in most regions.  
Timing may vary slightly, and a transport fee could apply depending on distance.
