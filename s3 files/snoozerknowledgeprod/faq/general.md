## ❓ How long does delivery take?
Most deliveries arrive within **3 to 7 business days** once your order is confirmed.  
You’ll receive a text or email with tracking information and scheduling options.

---

## ❓ Do you offer setup or removal?
Yes! You can add **White-Glove Setup** at checkout for in-room setup and packaging removal.  
We’ll also remove your old mattress when we deliver your new one.

---

## ❓ What’s the return policy?
Every mattress includes a **100-night sleep trial**.  
If it’s not the right fit, you can exchange it once or return it for a full refund within that window.  
*Note: motion bases, bedding, and pillows are final sale and not eligible for return.*

---

## ❓ How fast are refunds processed?
Refunds are typically processed within **3–5 business days** after the returned item is picked up.

---

## ❓ Do you offer financing or payment plans?
Yes! Flexible payment options are available through **Synchrony** and **Shop Pay Installments**, including **0% APR plans** for qualified customers.

---

## ❓ Are your prices the same online and in the showroom?
Yes — our pricing is consistent everywhere.  
However, you may earn **bonus rewards or limited-time discounts** during in-showroom events.

---

## ❓ How does your loyalty program work?
Our **Loyalty & Rewards Program** lets you earn points for purchases, referrals, and even for trying products in-store.  
Points can be redeemed for discounts, upgrades, or exclusive experiences.

---

## ❓ How long is the warranty?
All mattresses include a **10-year limited warranty** against defects in materials and workmanship.  
Adjustable bases carry a **5-year mechanical warranty**.  
Contact our support team if you ever need to start a claim.

---

## ❓ What’s the minimum purchase for financing or delivery perks?
Financing typically starts at **$499**.  
Orders **$999+** qualify for **free basic delivery** within our local service area.

---

## ❓ Do you charge for exchanges?
Exchanges are free within the trial period, and we’ll handle pickup at no cost.  
You can exchange once per purchase.
