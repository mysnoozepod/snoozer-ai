## ❓ What is the Rewards & Loyalty Program?
Our Loyalty Program lets you earn points for purchases, referrals, and in-showroom engagement.  
Points can be redeemed for discounts, upgrades, or exclusive experiences.

---

## ❓ How do I earn points?
- Every purchase earns points automatically.  
- You can earn additional points by visiting the showroom, completing the Snooze Assessment, or referring friends.  
- Points also accumulate during special promotions and events.

---

## ❓ How can I redeem my rewards?
You can redeem points directly through Snoozer or in-store at checkout.  
Just mention your account, and your available balance will apply toward eligible items.

---

## ❓ Do rewards expire?
Points remain active for **12 months** from your last purchase or showroom visit.  
You’ll receive reminders before they expire.

---

## ❓ Can I combine rewards with discounts or financing?
Yes — rewards can be combined with most sales or financing offers unless stated otherwise.

---

## ❓ Do I need to create an account?
Yes, you’ll need a simple Snoozer Rewards account to track your points.  
You can register in-store, through the website, or within Snoozer itself.

---

## ❓ What are showroom-exclusive offers?
During your in-person visit, you may unlock **extra rewards points** or **exclusive discounts** tied to your Snooze Session or product demos.
