## ❓ How long is the warranty?
All mattresses include a **10-year limited warranty** covering materials and craftsmanship.  
Adjustable bases typically include a **5-year mechanical and electrical warranty**.

---

## ❓ What does the warranty cover?
The warranty protects against **defects in materials and workmanship**, including excessive sagging or uneven wear that isn’t caused by misuse.  
If an issue qualifies, we’ll repair or replace the product at no charge.

---

## ❓ What isn’t covered by the warranty?
Normal softening, stains, misuse, or damage caused by improper support are not covered.  
Comfort preference changes after the trial period are also excluded from warranty coverage.

---

## ❓ Do I need to register my mattress or base?
No registration is needed.  
Your proof of purchase automatically activates your warranty coverage.

---

## ❓ How do I file a warranty claim?
If you ever experience an issue, contact us through Snoozer, by email, or in-store.  
We’ll guide you through a quick inspection and confirm next steps for repair or replacement.

---

## ❓ Is the warranty transferable?
No — coverage applies only to the original purchaser and can’t be transferred to another owner.

---

## ❓ What happens if my product can’t be repaired?
If a replacement is necessary, we’ll provide an equivalent model or credit toward a new product.  
Your remaining warranty term will continue from your original purchase date.

---

## ❓ Does the warranty include pickup or delivery?
Yes — if a product is replaced or serviced under warranty, we’ll coordinate delivery or pickup at no extra cost within our primary service area.
