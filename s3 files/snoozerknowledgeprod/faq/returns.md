## ❓ What’s your return policy?
All mattresses come with a **100-night sleep trial**.  
If you’re not completely satisfied, you can exchange once or return for a full refund within that period.  
Our team handles the pickup and refund process so there’s no hassle for you.

---

## ❓ Which items are not returnable?
For hygiene and safety reasons, **motion bases, adjustable frames, bedding, and pillows are final sale** and can’t be returned or exchanged once delivered or opened.

---

## ❓ How do I start a return?
Just contact us through Snoozer, your online order page, or in-store.  
We’ll schedule a pickup and confirm your refund details by email.

---

## ❓ Do I need to re-box or ship the mattress myself?
Nope — our delivery team handles pickup, packaging, and transport for you.  
All you need to do is schedule your preferred time.

---

## ❓ How long do refunds take?
Refunds are typically processed within **3–5 business days** after pickup.  
We’ll notify you as soon as it’s complete.

---

## ❓ Can I exchange instead of returning?
Yes — you can make **one exchange** within the trial period if you’d like to try a different comfort level or model.  
The new trial continues from your original delivery date.

---

## ❓ When does the 100-night trial start?
Your trial begins the day your mattress is delivered.  
We recommend keeping the mattress for at least **21 nights** before deciding, since your body may need time to adjust.

---

## ❓ Are there fees for returns or exchanges?
No fees within our primary delivery zone.  
If you’re outside our standard service area, a small pickup charge may apply to cover transport.
