## Product content acceptance rule

No item is ready for online publication until its commercial data is authoritative in Shopify and its physical experience coverage is recorded. Provisional names, quantities, or model families are not sufficient substitutes for exact variants, approved prices, vendor-confirmed availability, compatibility, fulfillment facts, and owner-approved customer-facing content.
