## 4. What if I sleep hot?

If you sleep hot, cooling features in the mattress can help, but the full sleep setup matters too. I would look for a mattress with cooling materials, then pair it with breathable sheets, cooling pillows, or other cooling accessories. The goal is to reduce heat buildup around your body, not just pick the coolest-sounding mattress.

Do not promise a mattress will eliminate heat issues.

## 5. What if I have back pain?

I am sorry to hear that. With back pain, stability and support matter most. If it is lower back pain, I would usually start with a mattress that gives you firmer support so your hips and lower back do not sink too far. If it is middle or upper back discomfort, a medium-firm feel may be more comfortable, especially if you sleep on your side.

I cannot diagnose the cause of back pain, but I can help you compare which mattress feels most stable and supportive while you test it.

## 6. I sleep on my side. What should I look for?

As a side sleeper, pressure relief is the big thing. Your shoulders and hips carry more pressure when you sleep on your side, so you want enough cushioning to reduce pressure in those areas. At the same time, the mattress still needs enough support to keep your spine from dipping out of alignment.

## 7. My partner moves a lot. What matters?

Motion separation matters most. All-foam mattresses generally do a strong job limiting motion transfer because the foam absorbs movement. Our hybrid mattresses can also help because they use pocketed coils, which move more independently than traditional connected coils.

If your partner moves a lot, I would test how much movement you feel when they shift positions, not just how the mattress feels when you lie still.

## 8. Do I need an adjustable base?

I strongly recommend considering an adjustable base because it gives you more control over your comfort and lets you try different sleep positions. It can be especially helpful if you like elevating your head, legs, or changing positions throughout the night.

That said, our mattresses are designed to be comfortable with or without an adjustable base. The base enhances the experience, but it is not required for the mattress to perform well.
